# Tala — domain language

- **Note**: a titled item in the Library made of one or more Pages. _Avoid_: notebook, document.
- **Page**: one fixed-size sheet of a Note holding its own typed text and Ink, optionally over a PDF page.
- **Ink**: handwriting drawn on a Page with the stylus. _Avoid_: drawing, scribble.
- **Library**: everything saved: Notes, Folders, Tags.
- **Bituin**: the star mascot who acts as the study coach. _Avoid_: assistant, bot.
- **Session**: continuous work on one Note; ends after 10 minutes without writing or when the Note is closed.
- **Study day**: a calendar day with at least 5 minutes of writing or marking up.
- **Weekly goal**: the number of Study days Bituin cheers toward each week; missing days resets nothing.
- **Wrap-up**: Bituin's short summary at the end of a Session.
- **Quiet mode**: the setting that silences Bituin's reactions and reminders.
- **Recording**: lecture audio captured while writing, lined up with Ink by time.
- **Task**: a checklist item in a Page's typed text; the Tasks tab lists them across the Library.
- **Backup**: a `.tala` file holding the Library.
- **Entry**: a typed line Tala understands because it starts with a marker (`P150 lunch`, `@ fri 2pm dentist`, `[ ] essay due fri`, `✓ water`); the line itself stays the record. _Avoid_: record, item.
- **Journal**: the note for one month whose Pages are its days; quick capture writes to today's Page.
- **Today**: the Home view: what the Library's Entries put on today, plus undated open Tasks.
- **Upcoming**: the next days of the Agenda, then every Due-again item.
- **Due-again**: an Entry repeated from when it was last done (`@ haircut every ~6w`), not on fixed dates.
- **Reminder**: an alert asked for on an Entry with `!` or `!15m`; Bituin never sends one.
- **Account**: where money sits (Cash, GCash, BPI), named in a line by its one word (`P150 lunch gcash`).
- **Safe to spend**: today's share of what the Accounts hold, less what is kept aside and the bills due before the next income.
- **Habit**: something kept up on a number of days a week, ticked with `✓ name` lines; Study is the first, counted from writing time.
- **Strength**: how steadily a Habit is kept, 0–100%; a miss dents it, nothing resets it.
- **Day off**: `✓ gym skip`, a day that leaves a Habit's Strength alone. _Avoid_: missed day.
